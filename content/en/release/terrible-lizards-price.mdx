## What Is Terrible Lizards?

Terrible Lizards is a first-person horror parkour game from developer WDR Studios LLC, published by Digital Pajamas and Behaviour Interactive Inc. It drops you into 1992 at Dalton's Dinos, a fading Route 66 roadside attraction baking under the New Mexico sun. The staff have vanished, a telephone keeps ringing with a plea to find a missing child, and your car is dead miles from anywhere.

The hook is straightforward: something is hunting you, and you cannot fight back. There are no weapons. Your only tools are speed, momentum, and the terrain itself — running, jumping, climbing, wall running, bouncing, and sliding through an abandoned dinosaur depot while creatures shamble and charge behind you.
