## FAQ

### What is the Terrible Lizards plot about?

The Terrible Lizards plot follows a traveler whose car breaks down on Route 66 in the New Mexico desert in 1992. With no phone and no nearby help, they take shelter in an old roadside dinosaur attraction — and discover that something dark and sinister is lurking below it.

### When does the Terrible Lizards plot take place?

It takes place in 1992. That date is deliberate: it predates widespread cell phone use, which means the protagonist has no realistic way to call for help. The isolation is baked into the era, not just the location.

### Is the Terrible Lizards plot based on a true story?

There's no evidence that the Terrible Lizards plot adapts a specific real event. It draws on real cultural touchstones instead — the golden age of Route 66 road trips and the faded dinosaur attractions that once lined it.

### Do I need to know the Terrible Lizards plot before playing?

No. The story is designed to unfold through exploration, so going in cold is arguably the best way to experience it. Just move slowly, read the environment, and let the Terrible Lizards plot reveal itself at its own pace.
