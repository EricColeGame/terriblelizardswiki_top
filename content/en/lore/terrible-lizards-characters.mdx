## Meet Jordan: The Protagonist at the Center of It All

Jordan is the anchor of the entire narrative. The official cast reveal describes him as a former track athlete, which is a quietly loaded detail in a survival-horror setup.

A trained runner stranded at night has options a typical horror victim does not: speed, stamina, and body awareness. But athleticism does not translate into wilderness knowledge or creature behavior. That tension — a body built for one kind of challenge dropped into a completely different one — is the most interesting thing about him on paper.

Kellen Goff is a seasoned presence in animation and game voice work, which suggests Jordan will be expressive rather than stoic. Treat that as inference, not confirmed direction.

### Why the Athlete Framing Matters

- **Physical capability creates stakes.** If Jordan can run, the threat has to be faster, smarter, or patient.
- **It sets up a false sense of safety.** Fitness feels like an advantage until the terrain and the dark take it away.
- **It gives the actor something physical to play.** Breath, pace, and panic become characterization tools.

## The Dinosaurs Themselves as Characters

In a project named after the literal translation of "Dinosauria" — the term Richard Owen coined in 1842 for the group we now call dinosaurs — the creatures are not props. They are the other half of the cast.

Community documentation notes that all characters, creatures, and props are computer animated, and that more than 15 character models were created for the production. That volume matters: a large model library usually signals that individual creatures get distinct silhouettes and behaviors rather than functioning as a generic swarm.

Armsby's earlier dinosaur shorts lean heavily on behavior, weight, and body language rather than dialogue. If that design language carries over, expect the creatures to be characterized through motion and sound design instead of exposition.

| Craft Element | Why It Matters for Character |

## FAQ

**Who is the main character in Terrible Lizards?**
Jordan, voiced by Kellen Goff, is the protagonist. He is described as a former track athlete whose car breaks down on an open road at night, leaving him stranded near something dangerous.

**How many Terrible Lizards characters are there?**
Community documentation reports that over 15 character models were created for the production. On the human side, four voice performers are credited, but only Jordan's role has been publicly described.

**Is Terrible Lizards connected to Dinosauria?**
Both projects are linked to animator David James Armsby, and they share a focus on behavior-driven dinosaur storytelling. A direct canonical connection has not been officially confirmed, so treat it as a strong but unverified assumption.

**Do the dinosaurs talk in Terrible Lizards?**
There is no indication that the creatures speak. Based on the creator's earlier work and the fully animated approach, the Terrible Lizards characters on the creature side are far more likely to be defined by movement, sound, and presence than by dialogue.
